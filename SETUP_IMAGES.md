# ClareVi Product Images Setup

The site is now configured to use real product images. To complete the setup, save your product photos to the `images/` folder with these filenames:

## Required Images

1. **images/magic-circle.jpg** - Rainbow spring candy product photo
   - This shows the Magic Circle (rainbow spring with fruit candy)
   - The image from the prompt showing the colorful spiral springs box

2. **images/comb-pop.jpg** - Comb pop candy product photo
   - This shows the Comb Pop (colorful comb toys with candy)
   - The image from the prompt showing the comb toys in their display box

3. **images/crazy-shot.jpg** - Chocolate shot candy product photo
   - This shows the Crazy Shot (chocolate syringes)
   - The image from the prompt showing the colored chocolate shot tubes

4. **images/mix-bag.jpg** - Mixed bag product photo
   - This shows The Mix Bag (combination of all three products)
   - Can use a photo showing all products together, or any combination

## Steps

1. Take or source clear, well-lit photos of each product
2. Optimize the images to be web-friendly (JPG format, ideally 600x600px or similar)
3. Save them to the `images/` folder with the exact filenames listed above
4. The site will automatically load and display them

## Image Tips

- Use clear, bright lighting
- Show the product attractively and realistically
- JPG format works best for product photos
- Aim for images that are roughly square (or landscape) for best appearance in the card layout
- Make sure the candy/product is the main focus of the image
