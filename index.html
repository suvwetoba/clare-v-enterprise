Run by a teenager who actually eats the stock

Clare V enterprise started the way most small shops do: buying one carton, selling it to friends at school, then buying two. Every sweet on this page is one she picked herself because people kept asking for it.

Everything is sold sealed in its original wrapper, straight from the carton — you can see the batch and expiry printed on the pack when it reaches you. The Crazy Shot chocolate carries NAFDAC registration no. A5-101255 on the box.

Questions

How do I pay?

Bank transfer or cash. Clare V sends account details on WhatsApp once she confirms your order — never enter card details on this page, because it doesn't take them.

Is there a minimum order?

No minimum for school pickup — one sweet is fine. For delivery, orders under ₦2,000 still pay the full delivery fee, so it's usually worth adding a few more.

Can I order a full carton for a birthday?

Yes, and it's the cheapest way to buy. Full-box prices are on every product above. For anything bigger than a carton, message her and she'll quote you.

Are these safe for small children?

The toy items — Magic Circle springs and Comb Pops — have small parts, so they suit ages 4 and up with an adult around. Crazy Shot is a soft chocolate cream and is fine for younger children.

What if something arrives damaged?

Send a photo the same day and she'll replace it on the next delivery. Sealed packs only — she can't take back anything already opened.
